Open Circuits

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-13

No data collected

Open Circuits does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. No information about you, your device, or your usage ever leaves your device.

No network access

The app has no internet permission and makes no network requests of any kind. It works entirely offline. There are no servers to send data to even if we wanted to.

What stays on your device

Your bookmarks and settings are saved in the browser's local storage inside the app's WebView. This data never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the app.

Third parties

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporters, and no tracking of any kind. No third party receives any data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the project maintainer via the issue tracker.
