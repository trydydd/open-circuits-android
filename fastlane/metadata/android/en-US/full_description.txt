Open Circuits is a self-contained electronics reference that runs entirely on your device. No internet connection is ever required.

The content covers six volumes of practical electronics: DC fundamentals, AC circuits, semiconductors, digital logic, signals, and power. Each chapter includes worked examples and circuit diagrams rendered from the bundled HTML.

What it does:
- Covers resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, transistors, op-amps, logic gates, and more
- Full-text search across all chapters
- Bookmarks and reading position saved between sessions
- Works in airplane mode, on the subway, in the field

What it does not do:
- No internet requests. The app holds no INTERNET permission.
- No tracking, no analytics, no crash reporting
- No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync
- No ads

The app is a thin native wrapper around the open-circuits HTML content. The WebView loads the bundled site from local assets; no data leaves the device.

Source code is on GitHub under GPL-3.0. Builds are reproducible: two clean builds of the same source produce the same APK bytes.

Compatible with Android 8.0 and later.
